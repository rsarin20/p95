What you are playing against

flybody is an anatomically detailed model of the fruit fly, built by Google DeepMind with HHMI Janelia and published in Nature in 2025. The body on screen is that model: 68 segments, 109 degrees of freedom, 78 actuators, a measured mass of 0.98 mg. Its outline here is the real visual mesh, projected through this camera at 16 headings.

The opponent's stroke was trained, not scripted. A small network reads the ball and commands flight acceleration, paddle yaw and pitch, and a swing; an evolution strategy optimised it over millions of simulated rallies until the returns landed on your half. The panel above is its six live outputs.

The table is 4.4 cm long — fly scale. At that size a rally takes about a tenth of a second, which is why the chamber runs in slow motion.
